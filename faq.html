How does End-of-Day vs. Intraday Drawdown work?

The Growth Plan is End-of-Day (6%): your trailing drawdown floor only moves up at market close. The Express Plan is Intraday (5%): the floor updates in real time with your highest equity peak. On all plans, breaching the floor during the session closes the account (or lets you use a 10%-off Discounted Reset).

What counts as a Qualifying Winning Day for payouts?

A payout requires 8 non-consecutive qualifying winning days in the current payout cycle. A qualifying day is any trading day you finish with realized profit of at least $200 on a 100K account, $150 on a 50K, or $100 on a 25K.

Are overnight holds and 0DTE options allowed?

Yes! Overnight positions are allowed on all accounts and in every phase. Expiring same-day positions must be closed before 3:55:00 PM ET (or 4:10:00 PM ET for SPY, QQQ, IWM, DIA) or they are automatically closed to protect you from assignment risk.

Is the $129 Funded Activation Fee really refunded?

Yes. The moment you take your first payout on a Funded account, the flat $129 activation fee is added back on top of your payout—so once you are profitable, activation costs you nothing.
