[ 01 ] — Manifesto
M / 001

Architecture is the
thoughtful making
of space.

We reject ornament. We reject the decorative impulse. Our work begins with a single block — a monolith — and proceeds by subtraction. What remains is not less, but essential.

Each project is a study in the relationship between concrete and light: how mass catches shadow, how shadow defines form, how form organizes silence. We build for those who understand that restraint is the highest luxury.

[ 03 ] — Studio

A practice of
twelve minds.

Founded in Berlin in 2014 by Klaus Vasari and Yumi Tanaka, MONOLITH operates from a converted industrial warehouse in Kreuzberg, with a satellite studio in Tokyo's Shimokitazawa district.

The studio works intentionally small — twelve architects, three materials researchers, and a rotating cast of collaborators. We take on no more than four projects per year.

[ 04 ] — Process
i.
EXCAVATE

Site analysis, historical context, material sourcing. We dig before we draw.

ii.
SUBTRACT

From a single monolithic mass, we remove. Form emerges through negation.

iii.
ILLUMINATE

Light studies, solar analysis, shadow mapping. We design with the sun as collaborator.

iv.
REALIZE

Construction documentation, on-site supervision. We build what we draw.
