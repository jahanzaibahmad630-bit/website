kissaki — The Point

Where the edge turns back on itself in one sweeping arc. The bōshi — the temper’s own signature inside the tip — is the hardest thing for a smith to hide; here the steel confesses its maker.

01

玉鋼

Tamahagane — Jewel Steel

Smelted from iron sand in a clay tatara furnace over three days and nights. The smith sorts the bloom by sound and by colour, keeping only the bright, brittle hearts of pure steel.

02

折返鍛錬

Orikaeshi — The Folding

The block is heated, hammered flat and folded — fifteen times over. Thirty-two thousand seven hundred and sixty-eight layers. Impurity leaves with every fold; the memory of it stays in the grain.

03

素延し

Sunobe — Rough Shaping

The bar is drawn out into the silhouette of a blade: curvature, ridge, point. What leaves this stage can never again become anything other than a sword.

04

焼入れ

Yaki-ire — The Quench

Clay, thin at the edge and thick at the spine. Heated to the colour of the morning sun, then plunged into water. The edge screams into hardness while the spine holds soft — the hamon is born here, or never.

05

研ぎ

Togi — The Polish

Twelve stones, from coarse to mirror, weeks in the hands of a polisher alone. The hamon is never painted on — it is uncovered, the way dawn uncovers mist in a valley.
