How do payouts work?

Growth EOD vs Express Intraday Drawdown?

Growth Plan (6% trailing drawdown) only updates your drawdown floor at market close (End-of-Day) and allows multi-leg writing (Iron Condors, Verticals). Express Plan (5% drawdown) trails intraday peak equity and is Buy-Only.

Can I hold options overnight?
