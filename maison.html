Chapter I • The Provençal Cradle

Where Morning Mist Meets
Sunken Valley Air

“In Grasse, the soil does not merely feed the flower; it imparts centuries of geological memory into every single drop of nectar.”

Perched between the Mediterranean coastline and the maritime foothills of the Southern Prealps, Grasse possesses an incomparable microclimate. Here, warm maritime breezes collide with cool alpine currents, coaxing botanicals into producing essential oils of unmatched olfactory depth.

Chapter II • The Artisanal Alchemy

The 180-Day Cold Maceration

While modern industrial perfumery relies on rapid thermal synthesis, LORÉN preserves the sacred slowness of classical French maceration.

ETAPE 01

Hydro-Distillation & Enfleurage

Fresh botanical harvests undergo low-pressure copper steam distillation and traditional cold enfleurage to extract volatile essence without thermal denaturation.

ETAPE 02

180-Day Oak Maturation

The concentrate rests in charred French oak casks deep within underground limestone vaults, allowing raw molecules to harmonize into liquid velvet over six months.

ETAPE 03

Sub-Zero Glaciation (-4°C)

Before bottling, the macerated extrait is chilled to -4°C. Natural plant waxes precipitate out gently, resulting in crystalline clarity without stripping aromatic potency.

ETAPE 04

32% Extrait Hand-Pouring

Each flacon is individually filled at pure parfum concentration (32%), sealed with silk cord, and inspected under directional light by master artisans in Grasse.
