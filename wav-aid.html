Wav-Aid UI/UX Architecture

This interactive prototype addresses every requirement outlined in your 10-screen design document. Built with a dark studio aesthetic, responsive AI visualizers, and tactile vinyl record components.

01. Studio Palette

Pure black (#08080A), charcoal glass, with subtle lavender/purple AI accents. Zero aggressive neon.

02. AI Particle Sphere

Replaced flat waveforms with an organic, voice-reactive canvas sphere in the Recording view.

03. Rotating Vinyl Engine

Custom CSS spinning vinyl records with center labels and realistic grooves for takes and session cards.

04. Multi-Take Crown

Automatic crown highlight and algorithmic pitch consistency rating for the best vocal take.
