Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your DelveDeep journey.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?
We understand that life shifts. You may cancel with a full refund (minus a $150 administrative processing fee) up to 45 days before retreat commencement (August 30, 2026). Between 44 and 21 days, deposits can be transferred to any future 2027 DelveDeep sanctuary date. Within 20 days, payments are non-refundable due to boutique venue commitments.
Do I need prior breathwork or meditation experience?
No prior experience is necessary. All somatic sessions are structured with trauma-informed progression. Our facilitators offer continuous individual adjustments, and you have complete bodily autonomy to pause or soften your practice at any time.
How do I get to the sanctuary in Costa Rica?
Fly into Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) in San José, followed by a scenic 45-minute domestic hopper flight via Sansa Airlines directly to Puerto Jiménez (PJM). Our DelveDeep private Mercedes Sprinter shuttle will greet you on the tarmac and transport you directly into the rainforest reserve.
How does Kajabi process my reservation?
When you submit your registration, your customer details are transferred to our secure Kajabi checkout portal. Once processed, you receive an automated confirmation email with your official booking voucher, receipt, packing checklist, and calendar invites.
